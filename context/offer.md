# FootHold — Offer

*The three tiers, pricing logic, proof, and claims to avoid*

> [!info] How to read this
> **Known** = observed in the code or stated directly by Max.
> **Assumed** = a hypothesis that reached live copy without customer research.
> `[UNSURE]` = genuinely unknown, left blank rather than guessed at.

### Packages

**Tier 1 — $49. "Here's how to fix what the scan found."**
The prioritised fixes from their own scan. Sold on the report page and in the
report email, at the moment the fixes are blurred in front of them. Impulse
purchase at peak context.

**Tier 2 — $1,500, one-off. Two to three weeks.**
- Every technical fix from the scan, implemented on the existing site
- Pages rewritten, positioning decided first, in their voice
- **Listing consistency**: Google Business Profile, trade directories and
  profiles they already own, made to agree on name, address, phone and claims
- **A second site on its own domain**, built for machines only
- They keep everything, both domains plus a written record of what changed

**Tier 3 — $2,000 up front, then $2,500/month, six-month minimum.**
Separate funnel. Only sold to existing $1,500 customers.
- 2x social posts from influencers AI is listening to
- High-trust `.gov` backlinks
- Monthly query tracking
- New answer content monthly
- Review generation system with an SOP for staff
- Monthly high-trust Reddit posts
- **Guarantee:** not ranking on LLM engines after 180 days → Max pays $15,000

### Pricing logic

- **$49** is priced as an impulse, below the threshold where anyone needs
  approval. Framed as less than an hour of most people's billable time.
- **$1,500** is one-off on purpose. "Not a retainer" is a selling point against
  agencies. Framed against the value of a single commercial job.
- **Tier 3** is where recurring work lives, because recurring work has recurring
  cost. The guarantee is affordable precisely because it only applies to people
  who already did tier 2.

**The tiers must never be blurred.** Monthly tracking, new content, link
acquisition and review systems belong to tier 3 only. Putting any of them in the
$1,500 promises ongoing work for a one-off fee. This has already been caught
once in the copy.

### Proof

Thin, and worth being honest about.

- **Known:** footholdsystems.com scored **20/100, grade F** on its own scan.
  Reproducible. Used as the opening of the email sequence.
- **Known:** foreverpartyrentals.com scored **91/100, grade B** as a local
  business, and **42/100, grade D** had it been scored as SaaS. Demonstrates the
  category system.
- **Known:** across roughly a dozen sites scanned, the developer-tooling checks
  were irrelevant to every non-software business.
- `[UNSURE]` No testimonials. No case studies. No before/after ranking data. No
  client results of any kind.

### Promises

- A written report showing what the answer engines say, who is named instead,
  and which signals are failing.
- No call required, no card, for the scan.
- Tier 2: implementation in two to three weeks, everything handed over.
- Tier 3 only: the $15,000 / 180-day guarantee.

### Claims to avoid

- **Any guaranteed ranking or recommendation.** The copy says outright that
  nobody controls a model's output and anyone claiming otherwise is lying. Never
  contradict this.
- **Invented statistics.** No fabricated percentages, adoption figures or
  "93% of buyers" claims. The site currently contains none, deliberately. If a
  number is used it must be sourced.
- **Monthly anything in tiers 1 or 2.**
- **Affiliation or partnership** with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Perplexity or
  Anthropic.
- **Overstating a scan result.** A partial pass is not a failure. Saying "your
  name doesn't bring up your website" to someone who ranks #1 destroyed trust
  once already.
- **Implying the scan reflects a personalised search.** It is a cold search: no
  login, no history, no location. The report says so.
- **Fabricated case studies or testimonials.** There are none. Do not write one.

### Good-fit customer

Yes:
- Real margin; one customer worth hundreds or thousands
- Customers research before buying
- Real business, real reviews, real work to point at
- Willing to move while the category is empty

No:
- Wants a guaranteed #1 in ChatGPT by Friday
- Would rather wait until it is proven and crowded
- Brand new site, no business or history behind it
- Wants a report to file, not changes to make
